Competition overview

Bring your research to the poster stage.

The competition has two phases: initial registration and abstract review, followed by the final poster presentation round for qualified teams.

01

Register your team

DIU students only. A team may include up to three members. Abstract submission closes on 11 November 2026 at 11:59 PM.

02

Abstract review

Submitted abstracts will be reviewed, and the results will be announced on 12 November 2026.

03

Final poster presentation

The final event is on 15 November 2026. Finalist teams must print their own A0 portrait poster (841 × 1189 mm).
