Competition overview

Bring your research to the poster stage.

The competition has two phases: initial registration and abstract review, followed by the final poster round for qualified teams.

01

Register your team

DIU students only. A team may include up to three members. Registration and abstract submission close on 2 November 2026.

02

Abstract review

Abstracts will be reviewed from 3–5 November. Approximately 25–30 qualified teams are expected to advance, subject to review and capacity.

03

Final poster round

The final event is planned for 15 November 2026. Finalist teams must print their own poster, at A0 portrait size (841 × 1189 mm).
