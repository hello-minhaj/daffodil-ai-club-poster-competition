Competition Rules

Review the eligibility, submission, and final-round requirements before submitting your team.

01

Eligibility and teams

  • Participation is open to Daffodil International University (DIU) students only.
  • Each team may have up to three members.
  • Team member details and DIU IDs should be entered accurately in the official registration form.
02

FYDP and thesis restriction

FYDP projects and thesis projects will not be accepted. Do not submit an FYDP or thesis project as your research poster competition entry.

03

Abstract submission

  • Submit the research title, research field/category, and abstract through the official Google Form.
  • The abstract must be 250–300 words.
  • Registration and abstract submission deadline: 7 November 2026, 11:59 PM.
04

Finalist poster requirements

  • Each finalist team is responsible for printing its own research poster.
  • Poster size: A0 portrait — 841 × 1189 mm.
05

Fees

  • Phase 1 registration fee: Tk 150 per team.
  • Qualified finalist teams pay an additional Phase 2 fee of Tk 350 per team.
  • Total fee for teams participating in both phases: Tk 500 per team.
06

Review and selection

Abstract review is planned for 8–9 November 2026. Approximately 25–30 teams are expected to qualify for the final round, subject to review and event capacity.
