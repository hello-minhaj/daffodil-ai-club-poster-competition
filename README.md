# Daffodil AI Club — Research Poster Competition Website

A responsive Django information website for the Daffodil AI Club Research Poster Competition. Team registration is handled through the official Google Form, so the website does not require organizers to maintain a separate participant-registration admin workflow.

## Website pages
- **Home:** organizer branding, event overview, countdown, key dates, participation fees, and registration links.
- **Competition Rules:** eligibility, abstract limit, FYDP/thesis restriction, finalist poster printing responsibility, minimum poster size, and phase fees.
- **Prize Pool:** cash awards and finalist certificate/recognition information.
- **Footer:** Daffodil AI Club social links, president/vice president contact details, CSE and DIU logos, and developer name linked to LinkedIn.
- Fixed blue navigation bar, responsive mobile menu, and AI Club event photo as a lightened hero background.

## Registration
All **Register your team** links open the organizer-supplied Google Form in a new tab:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfuHtoBq-NETjZJ-XuzV91aeC4-XSc3UylG9FeTZzdWs05B4g/viewform?usp=header

Google Form responses are managed in Google Forms/Google Sheets by the Club. This website itself does not collect registrations, store participant submissions, or provide a custom participant admin portal.
